Support

This page provides support and contact information for applications and services developed by Invidi Design.

Application Support

If you experience a problem with an Invidi Design application, have a question about its functionality, or need assistance with a connected service, please contact Invidi Design using the support email address associated with the application.

Privacy and Data Requests

Users may contact Invidi Design regarding privacy questions, information associated with an application, or requests for deletion of information maintained by an Invidi Design application.

When submitting a request, please identify the application involved and provide enough information for the request to be reviewed.

Google Account Access

Applications that integrate with Google services require authorization through Google's account permission system.

Users can review or revoke third-party application access through their Google Account security settings.

Contact Invidi Design

For application support, privacy questions, data deletion requests, or other inquiries, please contact:

Email: invididesign@gmail.com

Please include the name of the application in your message whenever possible.
