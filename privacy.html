Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how applications developed by Invidi Design may access, use, store, and protect information provided by users or connected services.

Last updated: August 16, 2026

1. Information We May Access

Applications developed by Invidi Design may request access to information from Google services or other connected platforms when that information is necessary to provide the application's intended functionality.

The specific information accessed depends on the permissions requested by the individual application and approved by the user.

Depending on the application, this may include basic Google account information or data associated with authorized Google services.

2. How Information Is Used

Information obtained through connected services is used only to provide, maintain, improve, or support the functionality requested by the user.

Invidi Design does not use Google user data for advertising purposes and does not sell Google user data to third parties.

3. Google API Services

Some Invidi Design applications may use Google APIs after a user explicitly grants permission through Google's authorization process.

Invidi Design's use and transfer to any other application of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Data Sharing

Invidi Design does not sell, rent, or trade personal information obtained through Google APIs.

Information may only be shared when necessary to provide application functionality, comply with applicable law, protect users or services from abuse, or when the user has provided appropriate authorization.

5. Data Storage and Security

Invidi Design takes reasonable measures to protect information handled by its applications from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

Some applications may process information temporarily without permanently storing it. When information must be stored, reasonable safeguards are used based on the nature of the data and the requirements of the application.

6. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the requested functionality, satisfy operational requirements, or meet applicable legal obligations.

Retention practices may vary depending on the individual application and the type of information involved.

7. User Control and Revocation

Users may revoke an application's access to their Google account through their Google Account security and permissions settings.

Revoking authorization prevents the application from obtaining additional information through the revoked authorization.

8. Data Deletion Requests

Users may request deletion of information associated with an Invidi Design application by contacting us through the Support page on this website.

Reasonable deletion requests will be processed subject to technical, legal, and operational requirements.

9. Third-Party Services

Invidi Design applications may interact with third-party services, including Google services. Those services operate under their own privacy policies and terms.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as applications, services, legal requirements, or data practices change.

Updates will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

11. Contact

Questions regarding this Privacy Policy or the handling of information by an Invidi Design application may be submitted through our Support page.
